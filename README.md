# Get Milk

This project is a fast-paced game about obtaining milk (the final objective). Bullets, enemies, boxes and you have physics.
This game may take a bit to load because of the map size. If it says "not responding" uhh please wait I guess...

You're just really thirsty and milk is the only thing that'll quench your thirst.
Shoot your way through enemies and parkour your way out of dangerous situations to finally attain that precious dairy product that you need.
Boxes are breakable btw.

Instructions:
A to move Left
D to move Right
Space to Jump
T to talk
M1 to shoot
